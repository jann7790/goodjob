#include <stdlib.h>  
int* twoSum(int* nums, int numsSize, int target, int* returnSize) {  
// 1. 設定回傳陣列的大小  
// LeetCode 的 C 語言介面要求我們透過指標 *returnSize 告訴呼叫者我們回傳了幾個數字。  
// 題目保證一定有解且為兩個數字，所以這裡設為 2。  
*returnSize = 2;  
// 2. 配置記憶體  
// 題目要求回傳的陣列必須是 malloc 出來的 (Heap Memory)，  
// 這樣離開函式後，陣列才不會消失。  
int *result = (int*)malloc(2 * sizeof(int));  
if (result == NULL) return NULL; // 安全檢查  
// 3. 雙重迴圈搜尋 (Brute Force)  
// 外層迴圈 i：代表第一個數字的位置  
for (int i = 0; i < numsSize; i++) {  
// 內層迴圈 j：代表第二個數字的位置  
// j 從 i + 1 開始，避免重複計算自己 (i != j) 且避免重複組合。  
for (int j = i + 1; j < numsSize; j++) {  
// 4. 判斷是否符合目標  
// 如果 陣列[i] + 陣列[j] 等於 target  
if (nums[i] + nums[j] == target) {  
result[0] = i;  
result[1] = j;  
return result; // 找到答案直接回傳  
}  
}  
}

return NULL;  
}

Start by submitting your application and resume through the company's online application system or by sending it directly to the human resources department.  
Resume Screening:  
The HR team will review the submitted resumes to identify candidates who meet the basic qualifications for the position.  
Initial Interview:  
Selected candidates may be invited for an initial interview, usually conducted by HR or a recruiter. This interview aims to assess your background, career goals, and overall fit for the company.  
Skills Assessment or Testing:  
Depending on the position, you may be asked to complete skills assessments, technical tests, or other evaluations to demonstrate your capabilities.  
Technical Interview:  
For positions requiring specific technical skills, a technical interview may be conducted. This stage assesses your proficiency in job-related tasks and problem-solving abilities.  
Panel Interview:  
Some companies conduct panel interviews where you meet with multiple team members or managers simultaneously. This allows the company to gather input from various perspectives.  
Behavioral Interview:  
Behavioral interviews focus on your past experiences and how you've handled certain situations. Be prepared to provide specific examples that demonstrate your skills and competencies.  
Final Interview:  
The final interview often involves meeting with higher-level executives or decision-makers within the company. This interview may cover your overall fit with the company's culture and values.  
Reference Check:  
The company may contact your provided references to verify your work history and qualifications.  
Job Offer:  
If you successfully pass all interview stages, the company may extend a job offer. This offer will include details about the position, salary, benefits, and other relevant information.  
Negotiation and Acceptance:  
Once you receive the job offer, there may be a negotiation phase where you discuss details such as salary, benefits, and start date. Upon reaching an agreement, you formally accept the job.  

#### 給其他面試者的中肯建議

Research the company and interviewers  
Understanding key information about the company you’re interviewing with can help you go into your interview with confidence. Using the company’s website, social media posts and recent press releases will provide a solid understanding of the company’s goals and how your background makes you a great fit.
